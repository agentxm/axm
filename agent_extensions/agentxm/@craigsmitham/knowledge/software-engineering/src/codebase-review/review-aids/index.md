# Codebase-review aids

Optional methods for locating, challenging, and interpreting evidence after a
review has selected product-quality criteria. These aids intentionally contain
the “how” that does not belong in the outcome lists. Select only what fits the
claim context, consequence, repository, and evidence access.

- [Repository evidence](repository-evidence.md) - Use when locating and interpreting evidence across source, configuration, history, generated artifacts, and dependency relationships without treating proxies as findings.
- [Scenario analysis](scenario-analysis.md) - Use when challenging product-quality claims with concrete stakeholder, workload, failure, threat, hazard, integration, and change scenarios.
- [Verification evidence](verification-evidence.md) - Use when selecting and interpreting tests, analysis, proofs, scans, measurements, and reviews as evidence rather than product outcomes.
- [Runtime investigation](runtime-investigation.md) - Use when a product-quality claim depends on execution, operational signals, representative workloads, degradation, interruption, or recovery.
- [Model-assisted review](model-assisted-review.md) - Use when one or more frontier models assist a codebase review; preserve bounded claims, attributable evidence, counterevidence, uncertainty, and human decision authority.
