# Spec

Produce the why-and-what artifact for one bounded Change without taking over
Change coordination, Change Design, or semantic authority.

This skill belongs to the Gen Stack pack. Resolve knowledge through active AXM
scope; in this source workspace read, in order:

- `knowledge/gen-stack/src/processes/running-change-realization-stages.md`;
- `knowledge/gen-stack/src/work-items/changes.md`; and
- `knowledge/gen-stack/src/work-items/writing-change-specifications.md`.

When the Change has an established Defect and explicit remedial purpose, also
read `knowledge/gen-stack/src/work-items/addressing-defects-through-changes.md`.
That condition classifies the Change as Bugfix; it does not change the artifact
type or template. Follow narrower linked Guides only when materially implicated.

## Boundary

Change Specification owns why and what: sources, outcome, scope, Intent,
Requirement and Architecture dispositions, semantic Requirement-satisfaction
and Architecture-realization Protocols, constraints, risks, unresolved
meaning, ratification state, and ratification request.

The Change owns identity, classification, exact artifact revisions, coherence,
delivery, evidence links, and next action. Change Design owns how, including
technical realization of required Protocols and optional Implementation-
conformance Evaluations.

A Pitch, current Implementation, test, prototype, or proposed mechanism is
evidence or provisional context, not accepted desired state. Return implied
outcome, obligation, durable boundary, decision, or semantic Protocol changes
to their authorities.

## Specify

1. Bind one Change, its source records, current classification, requested
   specification decision, meaning maturity, and authority.
2. Separate observations, assumptions, inferences, proposals,
   recommendations, accepted decisions, and unknowns.
3. State the current condition, implementation-independent intended outcome,
   scope, boundaries, and non-goals.
4. Give every affected Requirement and Architecture authority an exact
   disposition and complete ratifiable before-and-after meaning. Leave no
   architecture-significant choice for Design or implementation to infer.
5. Specify every required Requirement-satisfaction and Architecture-
   realization Protocol at the semantic level: identity, role, targets, claim,
   coverage, judgment, evidence expectations, lifecycle, authority, and
   blockers. Do not prescribe executable realization.
6. Record material constraints, invariants, risks, open decisions, decision
   owners, and the exact later actions each gap blocks.
7. Reconcile any supplied Change Design with the exact specification revision.
   Return semantic deltas to their owner without absorbing the Design.
8. Produce the canonical Change Specification artifact and stop. Do not claim
   overall Change coherence or coordinate delivery.

## Output

Use the semantic and representation contract in `Writing Change
Specifications`; do not reproduce or modify its heading inventory here.

- Prefer exact native host fields when they satisfy the contract.
- In a Markdown-only host or conversation, copy the Guide's exact canonical
  fallback, retain every top-level heading, and use `Not applicable` only when
  justified.
- Emit the same Change Specification whether or not the Change is classified
  as Bugfix.

Use only the maturity states and ratification rules the Guide defines. A draft
may be useful while a visible gap blocks ratification. A missing required
semantic Protocol blocks ratification, Change coherence, and dependent
planning. Ratification binds one exact revision but does not accept Design,
establish coherence, authorize implementation, or verify delivery.

Route a needed technical response to `design`, a combined explicitly requested
specification-and-design job to `quick-change`, a diagnostic gap to
`investigate`, and external evidence uncertainty to `research`.
