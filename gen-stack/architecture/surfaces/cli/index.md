# AXM CLI command surfaces

Accepted named command encounters beneath the AXM CLI Surface. Exact command
signatures and current inventory remain owned by the executable command tree.

- [Install command](install.md) - The `axm install` encounter for expressing
  direct extension intent and realizing affected workspace state.
- [Update command](update.md) - The `axm update` encounter for advancing or
  rematerializing an already desired extension.
- [Uninstall command](uninstall.md) - The `axm uninstall` encounter for removing
  direct extension intent while preserving other desired routes.
- [Sync command](sync.md) - The `axm sync` encounter for reconciling managed
  workspace state and owned outputs with desired state.
- [Lint command](lint.md) - The `axm lint` encounter for inspecting extension
  and workspace invariant violations.
- [Publish command](publish.md) - The `axm publish` encounter for validating and
  distributing workspace-authored extensions.
- [CLI help](help.md) - The connected command and topic discovery encounter for
  understanding AXM interfaces without executing them.
- [Pack authoring commands](packs.md) - The `axm packs add` and `axm packs
remove` encounters for editing authored Pack dependency intent.
