# Skill inventory hid physical outcomes at 80 columns

## Context

A real 80-column terminal displayed the Skill inventory during native-output verification.

## Friction

The long Potential readers column remained visible while Configured agents, Native locations, Discovery, and Agent outcomes were hidden. Summary counts remained visible but did not identify the affected locations or explain discovery uncertainty.

## Outcome

Marked the physical outcome, configured-agent, discovery, and outcome columns required, and the potential-reader catalog optional. Follow-up terminal inspection at 80 columns retained these facts; at 40 columns the existing vertical layout preserved all fields. No overflow was observed in those inspections.

## Target condition

A narrow Skill inventory keeps configured consumers and native outcomes visible, including duplicate-discovery uncertainty, before spending space on the catalog-wide reader list.
