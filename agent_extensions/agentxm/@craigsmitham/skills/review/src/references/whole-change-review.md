# Whole-change review

Apply this overlay in integrated final review and whenever a focused review
exposes a material cross-domain concern.

Inspect:

- exact Change Specification and Change Design reconciliation;
- plan, scope, and authorized-change completion;
- unexplained additions, omissions, and semantic expansion;
- migration, compatibility windows, rollout, rollback, and forward recovery;
- operational readiness, observability, and irreversible points;
- documentation and corpus coherence;
- candidate, authority, Execution, Result, and review provenance;
- stale checkpoint reviews and undispositioned findings;
- newly exposed Requirement, Architecture, Design, or Protocol gaps; and
- whether the claimed readiness is broader than the evidence.

This overlay detects a candidate that perfectly implements an incomplete or
incoherent definition. It may report and route that gap; it does not accept new
meaning or take over release authority.
