# Principle document

Use a principle document when people must make a recurring choice, the
circumstances vary, and they have grounds for a shared direction. The
document communicates a principle that readers can use to give reasons for
their choices. A decision record can then preserve how someone applied that
principle in one case.

## What makes it fit

A useful formulation connects a good worth protecting to a direction for
action. Its warrant tells readers why the claim deserves weight and who
recognizes it. Its strength matters. A professional commitment, a useful
default, and a requirement enacted by a particular organization place
different obligations on a practitioner. If every legitimate case has the
same prescribed answer, the reader may need the governing rule. To carry
out a settled procedure, they may need a [how-to guide](how-to.md).

A team can also draft a principle it wants to adopt. Until the people
responsible accept it, label the direction as a proposal. Readers deciding
one case need to know whether they can rely on a shared commitment or
whether agreement is itself still the decision before them.

The design has a cost. A short maxim travels easily and can acquire meanings
its authors never intended. A catalog of approved examples can make each new
decision look like a search for precedent. Supporting rationale and cases
earn their space when they show what changes the judgment and who requires
the knowledge or discretion to make it. A reader who can recite the maxim
and still cannot recognize a reason to depart from the usual choice needs
more than a memorable name.

## Examples and limits

The Agile Manifesto's authors connect frequent delivery with value for the
customer and also call for a sustainable pace. Together the commitments give
a team grounds to question a release plan that depends on repeated overtime.
That application is illustrative. The source establishes what its authors
advocate.[^agile]

Tim Peters’s *Zen of Python* puts short design preferences alongside
qualifications. Its informational standing is visible on the PEP itself.
Both documents show how a community can express durable direction compactly.
Neither establishes how well readers of a new principle document will use
it.[^zen]

For a [standard](../explainers/standard.md), look for a recognized basis of
assessment or coordination. A [practice](../explainers/practice.md) can
embody a principle even when nobody has written it down. A [playbook](playbook.md)
can help people choose responses within a specific family of situations. A
principle may guide several such responses. In a different domain,
O’Donovan’s account of public judgment distinguishes a general principle
from a decision made in particular institutional circumstances. The
portable distinction is between a direction and its application. The
authority for an actual professional decision belongs to its own setting.[^judgment]

[How principles guide judgment](../explainers/principle.md) follows a case
in which two reviewers can honor one commitment and recommend different
changes. To draft or revise the document readers will consult, follow
[How to write a principle document](../guides/writing-a-principle-document.md).
