name: experiment
description: Experiment within a codebase or a specified scope to reduce uncertainty about an engineering question through empirical evidence.
---

Experiment within the requested scope to reduce uncertainty about a
consequential engineering question or hypothesis through empirical evidence.

The result should make clear what was examined, under what conditions,
what was observed, and what the evidence supports for the intended
decision—including negative or inconclusive findings and remaining
uncertainty.

Let the decision's stakes and the user's constraints determine the extent
of investigation. Keep conclusions within the evidence's limits, and make
the status and limitations of experimental artifacts explicit.
