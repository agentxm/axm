# Spec-driven development (former harness route)

Specifications are durable or task-scoped context whose authority and
lifecycle must be explicit. Detailed ownership moved to context engineering.
Harness engineering is concerned with how specifications enter work, connect to
execution and verification, and remain observable to the agent. This route
remains for compatibility.
