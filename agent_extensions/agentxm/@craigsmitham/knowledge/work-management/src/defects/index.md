# Defect Reports

Concepts, guides, and a portable fallback template for observations, concerns,
and static findings that may indicate a defect.

- [Defects and Defect Reports](defects-and-defect-reports.md) - Explains how
  observations, failures, defects, reports, investigation, correction,
  verification, and closure remain distinct.
- [Recording Defect Reports](recording-defect-reports.md) - Use when an
  observation or concern may indicate a defect and needs an actionable,
  evidence-preserving work item.
- [Triaging Defect Reports](triaging-defect-reports.md) - Use when one or more
  Defect Reports need evidence-backed classification, relationship decisions,
  and a next route without invented diagnosis or priority.
- [Linking Defects to corrective Changes](linking-defects-to-corrective-changes.md) -
  Use when established defects and an authorized remedial purpose must remain
  traceable to a separate Change classified as Bugfix.
- [Defect Report template](defect-report-template.md) - Provides a compact,
  tracker-neutral body fallback that preserves discrepancy, conditions,
  evidence, impact, uncertainty, relationships, and next action.
