# Choosing readable examples

Use concrete examples to illustrate a rule.[^example-guide]
Examples alone do not establish correctness.[^example-guide]
Review examples with the rule's owner.[^review-notes]

[^example-guide]: [Example Guide](https://example.org/example-guide) explains that concrete examples illustrate rules and that examples alone do not establish correctness.
[^review-notes]: [Review Notes](https://example.org/review-notes) recommends reviewing examples with the rule's owner. Section 3 reports a secondary account of the workshop; the original workshop record was unavailable.
