# AXM ownership

## Accountable role

AXM maintainers collectively own maintenance of the public System and its
accepted System, Architecture, Requirement, decision, and assurance meaning.
Current people and assignments remain in GitHub rather than this concept.

## Stewardship boundary

Maintainers steward the public AXM CLI and OSS-safe reusable core through the
repository's accepted workflow. They do not own private AgentXM platform or
Registry responsibilities, third-party coding agents, external-provider
records, or decisions reserved to another explicit authority.

Repository or implementation access does not by itself grant authority to
accept new product meaning, Requirements, or durable Architecture.

## Continuity and escalation

Repository owners administer repository access, branch, and review controls and
provide continuity and escalation when maintainer authority is unavailable,
disputed, or changing. A transfer preserves the canonical corpus, unresolved
decisions, and available evidence without creating a parallel authority.
