Harden the shared ZIP central-directory reader. Archives must now end with exactly one end-of-central-directory record, keep their central directory directly before it, and declare entry counts that match. Multi-disk, ZIP64 and encrypted archives are rejected, as are entries whose local header disagrees with the central directory on name or compression method. Parsed entries now carry their creator version, CRC-32 and verified data offset.
