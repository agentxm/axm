# requirements-engineering

Portable concepts and guides for discovering, analyzing, specifying, reviewing,
changing, and maintaining requirements. The bundle governs requirement quality
without prescribing a delivery method, architecture framework, document form,
or requirements-management tool.

## Foundations

- [Foundations](foundations/) - The portable model, authority and maturity,
  artifact boundaries, verification and validation, and traceability model.
- [Development](development/) - Source discovery, candidate development,
  subject and level selection, and conflict resolution.

## Specification and assurance

- [Authoring](authoring/) - Content contract, classification, method selection,
  writing guidance, and a compact fallback template.
- [Review](review/) - Quality review for individual requirements and bounded
  requirement sets.
- [Lifecycle](lifecycle/) - Impact analysis, change specification, and stable
  identity and lineage.

## Local use

- [Adaptation](adaptation/) - Project-specific policy, host mapping, and
  optional composition with work management.
