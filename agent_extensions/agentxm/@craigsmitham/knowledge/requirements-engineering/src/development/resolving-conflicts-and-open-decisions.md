# Resolving conflicts and open decisions

Do not polish disagreement into an apparently settled requirement. Record the
conflicting claims, their sources, affected outcomes, and decision owner.

Frame a decision with:

- the exact question and why it matters now;
- viable options, including preserving the current state when relevant;
- evidence for and against each option;
- impact on stakeholders, qualities, constraints, cost, risk, and reversibility;
- a recommendation when the analyst has enough evidence;
- the authority, due point, result, rationale, and superseded candidates.

Unknowns may be resolved through research, modeling, prototyping, measurement,
negotiation, or an explicit risk acceptance. Until then, keep affected text
candidate or mark the normative requirement's unresolved field rather than
inventing certainty.
