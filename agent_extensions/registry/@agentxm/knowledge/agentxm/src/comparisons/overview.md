# Product comparisons

These comparisons explain products that overlap materially with AgentXM. They
help readers understand different product boundaries and choices; they are not
rankings, exhaustive market surveys, or substitutes for current vendor
documentation.

## Inclusion rule

A product is included when its current public product or technical
documentation treats Agent Skills or another AgentXM extension type as a
first-class managed artifact. The product may overlap with only part of
AgentXM, but that overlap must be intentional and supported, not merely
possible because the product can store arbitrary files.

A product is not included solely because it can store ZIP archives, OCI
artifacts, Git repositories, or other generic content. It may be reconsidered
if the vendor introduces an explicit agent-extension product or workflow.

## How to read the comparisons

Each comparison first describes the product in its own terms, then identifies
the durable overlap and boundary with AgentXM. Sourced product facts are kept
separate from AgentXM's interpretation of those facts.

These products change quickly. Each product comparison carries a review date
and favors stable responsibilities over detailed feature matrices. When a
comparison conflicts with current vendor documentation, the vendor's
documentation is authoritative for its product.
