# Engineering knowledge maintenance

Before changing this package, read the
[Agent extension enablement architecture](../../docs/architecture/extension-enablement.md).
It owns the collection's responsibility and its boundaries with AXM's product
contracts and derivative website material.

In a pattern, show the decision in an extension, what can vary, and when a
simpler choice suffices. In a guide, take a task from the author’s choice to
a result the consumer can check. Illustrate a principle with a choice it
changes. Give a reference a clear scope and an authoritative home. Write index
entries that help readers choose among destinations.

This file is repository maintainer guidance. Keep it outside the `src/` knowledge
bundle and excluded from publication.

Use the [Agent Extension Glossary](../../knowledge/axe/src/glossary.md)
for shared meanings. Keep definitions in that knowledge concept and maintain
consistent terminology in documentation, help, skills, and product copy.
