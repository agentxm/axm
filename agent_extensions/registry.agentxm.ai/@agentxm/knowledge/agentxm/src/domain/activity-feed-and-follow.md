# Activity Feed and Follow

The Activity Feed brings together recent changes relevant to a person or an
organization. It shows curated activity about extensions and libraries, and
names the library when an item appears because its extension is in one of your
libraries. It covers the last 90 days; it is not a permanent audit record.

## Where activity appears

- The personal **Feed** includes activity from what you follow and from people
  acting on your extensions and libraries. **All**, **Following**, and **Yours**
  narrow that view.
- An organization's **Feed** brings together relevant organizational activity,
  with an **In our libraries** view and a preview on its dashboard.
- A library's **Activity** tab shows its recent activity.

## Following extensions and libraries

A Follow is a person's private interest in an extension or library. It is
separate from a Favorite and from a paid Subscription. Following grants no
access: the [current visibility rules](visibility-and-discovery.md) still apply
to every activity and every subject it names.

Extensions offer **Feed only** and **Feed and email for new versions**.
Libraries offer **Feed only**. New-version email covers public versions, not
prereleases, and remains subject to notification preferences and access checks
at delivery.

The **Following** page lets you review your follows, change an extension's
level, or unfollow. Only you can see that list.

Whoever manages a public extension, library, or profile sees how many people
follow it, never who. The count updates weekly and appears only once 10 or more
people follow; below that, nothing is shown. Private extensions and libraries
show no count, and a subject made public counts only follows made since then.

If you lose access to a followed subject, its activity and its entry in
Following disappear. The follow becomes active again if access returns. Making a
subject public does not widen the audience of its earlier restricted activity.
