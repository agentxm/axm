# Design and change

Principles and patterns for deciding when software structure is justified and
how to introduce it in small, evidence-timed steps.

- [YAGNI: defer speculative capability and structure](yagni-and-speculative-complexity.md) — The principle of preserving optionality and economic timing by postponing premature commitment.
- [Tidy First](tidy-first.md) — A pattern for making a small behavior-preserving structural change immediately before the behavior change it enables.
