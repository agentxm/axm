# Operational Incident Records

Concepts, live-record guidance, and a portable fallback template for current or
imminent operational impact that requires coordinated response.

- [Operational Incident Records](operational-incident-records.md) - Explains
  how one living record coordinates impact, response, restoration, recovery,
  communication, closure, and independently owned follow-up.
- [Recording Operational Incidents](recording-operational-incidents.md) - Use
  when current or imminent service impact needs an attributable living record,
  clear control, chronology, handoff, and bounded exit conditions.
- [Operational Incident Record template](operational-incident-template.md) -
  Provides a current-state-first, tracker-neutral body fallback for operational
  impact and response coordination.
