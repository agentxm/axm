# Compare variants in context

Choose the unsettled decision and a case where it matters. Impeccable lets a designer compare variations of one element on the running page, keep one, or discard the set. Superpowers uses sketches for choices that benefit from visual comparison.[^generate][^sketch]

Suppose the author of an archive review skill wants findings that are easier to act on. Render the same finding in two candidate layouts inside the report. One leads with the user consequence, the other with the repair location. Keep the archive, evidence, and proposed fix identical so the reader can judge the ordering. Let them keep the original, choose a version, or request a specific adjustment.

Keep the cost of preview proportional to the decision. Two text samples can settle the order of a report. Rendering a longer preview is useful when surrounding content affects the choice. Keep previews disposable and identify what selecting one will change.

Try both choosing and discarding a variant. On selection, check that the chosen layout reaches the next draft with the original evidence intact. On discard, check that the initial artifact is still available unchanged.

[^generate]: Impeccable, comparing and accepting or discarding variations in the running page.

[^sketch]: Superpowers, visual comparison during brainstorming.
