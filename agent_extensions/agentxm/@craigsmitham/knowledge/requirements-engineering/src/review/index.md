# Reviewing requirements

Review may target one requirement or a declared set; the guides keep the scope
and strength of resulting claims explicit.

- [Reviewing individual requirements](reviewing-individual-requirements.md) -
  Provides a risk-sensitive review of one requirement's authority, content,
  quality, relationships, and assessment basis.
- [Reviewing requirement sets](reviewing-requirement-sets.md) - Reviews a
  bounded requirement set for consistency, coverage, balance, traceability, and
  changeability without claiming universal completeness.
