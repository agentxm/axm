# Shape

Use only after deliberate `$shape` selection. Natural-language similarity,
workflow position, or a completed prior activity does not activate this stage.
Selection grants no semantic, mutation, priority, or downstream-stage authority
beyond the artifact persistence events defined below.

Read these Gen Stack sources through active AXM scope; in this workspace read:

1. `knowledge/gen-stack/src/processes/running-change-realization-stages.md`;
2. `knowledge/gen-stack/src/processes/deciding-and-realizing-software-changes.md`;
3. `knowledge/gen-stack/src/control-loop/shaping-a-pitch.md`.

Repository-local accepted sources own system meaning.

## Boundary

Shape owns one provisional Pitch. It does not create accepted Intent,
Requirements, Architecture, Change Specification, Design, plan, implementation,
priority, estimate, or authorization.

It may identify anticipated Requirement-satisfaction and
Architecture-realization Protocol meaning. It must not prescribe test types,
files, Suites, fixtures, harnesses, commands, coverage, or
Implementation-conformance Evaluations.

## Work

1. Resolve the canonical Change target, host representation, current revision,
   permissions, and concurrency behavior.
2. Bind sources, evidence, decision roles, and available authority.
3. Separate the problem or opportunity from any supplied mechanism.
4. State the observable outcome, audience, appetite, boundaries, no-gos, and
   invariants.
5. Inspect the smallest sufficient repository and corpus neighborhood.
6. Identify only material anticipated Intent, Requirement, Architecture,
   Evaluation, Implementation, operational, Process, and Provenance impact.
7. Include a bounded filesystem or relationship sketch only when topology
   materially clarifies impact. Never invent paths, identifiers, or links.
8. Sketch rough response contours without selecting Design.
9. Put every next-acceptance blocker in `## Open items` using the common stable
   identity, authority role, and resolution-condition form.
10. Put a consequential human decision in Open items; keep the eligible next
    action in the shared handoff.

Choose the lightest truthful behavior: immediate Draft, Draft plus compact
elicitation, discovery first for high-consequence ambiguity, bounded research
or investigation, or termination when evidence supports deferment or no change.

## Stage completion

Apply the shared lifecycle, persistence, presentation, invalidation, and
compaction contract. A Pitch is Ready only when its framing is sufficient for
Specification, its exact current revision is verified in the canonical target,
and Open items contains `- None.`. `$spec`, not Shape, accepts that revision.

## Output

Follow the exact portable first-screen order, compact body, and readiness rules
in `Shaping a Pitch`.
Do not restore the former mandatory filesystem breadboard, exhaustive Impact
taxonomy, or Draft/Ready-for-response/Blocked/Deferred/No-change status list.
Deferment and no-change are stage terminations; a blocked Pitch is Draft with
Open items.

Stop after the Pitch or supported termination. Recommend the smallest eligible
explicit next stage without activating it.
