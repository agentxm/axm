# Field notes knowledge

Portable concepts for **observing work in progress and converting what you
observe into durable improvement**. The practice: declare a small number of
subjects worth watching, record specific incidents while the work is happening,
then periodically promote recurring incidents into verified changes.

This bundle defines the concepts. The `field-notes` rule performs capture; the
`field-notes` skill performs declaration and triage. Nothing here prescribes a
repository layout, tracker, or tool.

## Concepts

* [Field notes](field-notes-explainer.md) - What the practice is: the gap
  between work-as-imagined and work-as-done, why capture happens in-situ, why
  successful improvisation counts, and why notes and findings are separate.
* [Subjects](subject-explainer.md) - What a subject is: survey versus target
  mode, target conditions, how a survey subject graduates, and when a subject
  retires.
* [Closure](closure-explainer.md) - How notes become improvements: the
  recurrence threshold, promotion, verified closure, and the three ways
  observation systems fail.
