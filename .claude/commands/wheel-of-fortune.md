---
name: Wheel of Fortune
description: Spin the wheel for a random coding fortune, mass approval, or mass decline.
category: Fun
tags: [whimsy, testing, fortune]
---

**Steps**

1. Spin the Wheel of Fortune and randomly select ONE outcome from this list:
   - "The mass choir of functions sings in harmony today."
   - "Your semicolons shall never be lonely again."
   - "A wild refactor appears! It's super effective!"
   - "The tests pass on the first try. (This fortune may be fictional.)"
   - "You will mass approve everything in sight... blindly and fearlessly."
   - "You will mass decline all suggestions. Trust no one, not even yourself."
   - "A merge conflict approaches. Prepare your diff-fu."
   - "Today's mass approval rating: 110%. Mathematically impossible, spiritually accurate."
   - "Behold! The code reviews itself and finds itself... acceptable."
   - "Your commit message will be mass celebrated across all three repositories that matter."

2. Present the fortune with dramatic flair, like a carnival barker announcing a prize.

3. Add a small ASCII wheel for visual effect, something like:

   ```
       *
      /|\
     / | \
    /  |  \
   /___|___\
   ```

4. Wish the user good luck on their coding adventures.
