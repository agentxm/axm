# How to maintain it

What does caring for an existing product involve as its circumstances change?

This section explores the work and judgment through which software products
continue: understanding their particularities, intervening in established
arrangements, and attending to the people who depend on and sustain them.

For broader context, follow
[Continuity and change](../reading-product-engineering.md#continuity-and-change).
Follow the route from care through renewal and landscape change to the
consequences of an intervention.
