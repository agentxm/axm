---
name: align
description: Align a codebase or a specified part with supplied or identified requirements and guidance.
---

Align the requested scope with the applicable requirements and guidance:
its behavior, structure, and constraints should satisfy mandatory
requirements and reflect the intent of recommendations, with justified
departures made explicit.

Respect the sources' scope, authority, and permitted variation. Preserve
existing obligations that the alignment does not supersede.

The result should have evidence supporting its claimed alignment, with
remaining discrepancies, unresolved conflicts, and uncertainty made clear.
