# Developing requirements

These guides turn heterogeneous sources into analyzed requirement candidates
while preserving uncertainty and project decision authority.

- [Eliciting and inventorying requirement sources](eliciting-and-inventorying-requirement-sources.md) -
  Guides source discovery and elicitation while preserving provenance,
  uncertainty, and stakeholder differences.
- [Developing candidate requirements](developing-candidate-requirements.md) -
  Develops sourced observations and needs into bounded requirement candidates
  without implying acceptance.
- [Choosing requirement subject and level](choosing-requirement-subject-and-level.md) -
  Chooses an obligated subject and abstraction level appropriate to the
  decision without requiring a particular architecture taxonomy.
- [Resolving conflicts and open decisions](resolving-conflicts-and-open-decisions.md) -
  Preserves requirement conflicts, options, authority, and decision evidence
  instead of hiding uncertainty in wording.
